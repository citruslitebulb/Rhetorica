Rhetorica Privacy Policy

Rhetorica is offline-first. It does not create accounts or send your vocabulary activity to a Rhetorica server.

The app stores words, saved items, progress, and appearance settings on this device.

If Android Backup or device-to-device transfer is enabled on your phone, Google may copy that on-device data so you can restore Rhetorica after a setup or upgrade. Rhetorica does not operate its own backup service.

If you enable daily notifications, Android may show a Word of the Day reminder. You can change the time or turn this off in Settings.

If you choose a gallery image for the widget, Rhetorica reads that image only to draw the widget background.
